Frequently asked questions

Are the sessions online or on-site?

Both. Every course is delivered live and is available online (via Zoom) and on-site. When you register, you can choose the format that works best for you.

Is the course live or recorded?

All sessions are live instructor-led. Recordings may be shared with registered students at the instructor's discretion.

How do I pay?

After you register, you will receive a payment link by email. Payment methods include PayPal, Payoneer, and bank transfer.

Do I need experience to attend?

The Azure Master Course is intermediate. The AI & IT Fundamentals course is beginner-friendly and requires no technical background.

What if I miss a session?

Contact the instructor. In most cases you will receive materials or a recording of the missed session.

Do you offer corporate training?

Yes. Contact us for custom cohorts, private groups, or on-site delivery.
